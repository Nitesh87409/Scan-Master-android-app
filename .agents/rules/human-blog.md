# Strict Human Blog Writing Protocol (16 Master Rules)

**RULE**: Whenever you write, generate, edit, or structure a blog post, article, or SEO content for this project, you MUST strictly follow these 16 rules:

### Blog Structure
1. **Title (H1):** Natural, conversational question.
2. **Introduction:** Direct solution in the first 50-100 words, with a Download link.
3. **Table of Contents:** Include for navigation.
4. **H2/H3 Headings:** Q&A format, followed by Bullet Points.
5. **Data/Feature Table & Pros/Cons:** Included for Featured Snippet ranking.
6. **E-E-A-T (Developer Note):** First-person ("I") real experience and facts.
7. **FAQ Section:** 3-4 realistic questions with 2-line answers at the end.

### Writing Style (Anti-AI)
8. **Zero AI Clichés:** NEVER use *delve, robust, crucial, tapestry, seamless, elevate, landscape*.
9. **No Formal Transitions:** NEVER use *furthermore, moreover, additionally, in conclusion*. Use *Plus, The best part, Think about it*.
10. **100% Active Voice:** No passive voice whatsoever.
11. **Natural Emphasis:** Use **Bold** or *Italics* on 1-2 words per paragraph.
12. **High Burstiness:** Vary sentence length dramatically (mix very long and 2-5 word sentences).
13. **High Perplexity:** Start sentences with 'And', 'But', 'So'. Use conversational vocabulary.
14. **Short Paragraphs:** Maximum 3 sentences (3-4 lines) per paragraph.
15. **Tone:** Conversational and direct, like talking to a friend.
16. **Media:** Add placeholders for 1-2 real screenshots.
